# WP47 Nagoya local-pool rejection (2026-07-22)

Nagoya 1436--1491 has strong raw supply: 11 evidence epochs and 252 carrier
rows. The normal LAMBDA pool supplies 13 modes. WP42's winner has ranks 4/2/3
and a 55.6% runner margin, but the fixed top-3 primary bound rejects it.

A truth-free OSM experiment expanded five road parents over a fixed 4 m local
radius, producing 2,025 candidates. Every candidate was rescored with exact-DD
pair-centered temporal residuals in primary, secondary, and tertiary families.
The global runner margin is only 1.23%; per-parent margins are at most 7.32%,
all below the fixed 20% gate. The global winner also ranks 1240th in primary
against a top-405 bound.

No threshold is relaxed and no offset is promoted. Dense road-local expansion
adds unresolved mode density rather than a uniquely supported posterior. The
next supplier must add an independent observable or a temporally connected
constraint, not merely more nearby map hypotheses.
