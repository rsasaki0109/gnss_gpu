# WP48 Nagoya long-window and reverse-anchor rejection (2026-07-22)

Extending Nagoya 1436--1491 to 1436--1656 gives 44 evidence epochs and 1,011
carrier rows across 220 epochs. It still supplies only three fitted modes.
The WP42 winner ranks 1/3/1 against a top-1 family bound. Its runner margin is
exactly 20%, but the secondary-family rank rejects it. No long-window offset is
promoted.

A separate truth-free reverse trace starts from the accepted 1807--2327 static
anchor (candidate 49, clear wide-lane) and propagates to epoch 1436 using 345
TDCP and 26 late-bias Doppler intervals. The trace has 20.97 m target error,
20.98 m median error, and only one sub-50 cm epoch. It is rejected both as a
direct route and as an ambiguity candidate supplier.

Together these results show that more samples from the same temporal
observable and right-anchor reverse dead reckoning do not recover the missing
integer mode. The next experiment needs a genuinely independent absolute
position observable or a different ambiguity search parameterization.
