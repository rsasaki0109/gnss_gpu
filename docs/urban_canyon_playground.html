Urban Canyon Playground

A car drives east through a city at 18 km/h while you control the sky. Click satellites in the sky plot to block them — blocked satellites deliver a reflected (NLOS) signal whose range is biased long. Every change instantly re-solves all 60 epochs two ways with identical measurements: plain least squares vs. a Cauchy-weighted robust solver (the same IRLS idea as gnss_gpu.robust_spp). No install, no GPU — everything runs in your browser.

This is the browser twin of examples/demo_urban_canyon_sim.py (also available as a Colab notebook). Scaling this robust-weighting idea up — GPU particle filters, ray-traced NLOS against 3D city meshes — is what lets gnss_gpu beat RTKLIB demo5 on real UrbanNav data. Back to the results snapshot.
