PLATEAU Odaiba browser scene

City geometry: Project PLATEAU, Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport
and Tourism (Japan), Tokyo 23 wards 2022 CityGML, CC BY 4.0.
https://www.geospatial.jp/ckan/dataset/plateau-tokyo23ku-2022
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Modified by gnss_gpu contributors: highest LoD per feature component,
constrained triangulation preserving holes, reported GEOS linework repair,
geoid height conversion and ECEF-to-local-ENU transformation. No mesh
decimation or coordinate quantization. Original file hashes are in manifest.json.

Trajectory and navigation: UrbanNav-TK-20181219 Odaiba, IPNL-POLYU UrbanNavDataset.
https://github.com/IPNL-POLYU/UrbanNavDataset
MIT license and copyright notice: UrbanNav-LICENSE.txt in this directory.
Reference: Applanix POS LV620 reference.csv. All 12,410 ECEF poses retained.
Navigation: DLR/TUM CONGO/MGEX merged broadcast RINEX base.nav; GPS used here.

Height correction: Geospatial Information Authority of Japan (GSI),
Japan geoid 2011 (GSIGEO2011), version 2.1. Bilinear interpolation on the
one arc-minute by 1.5 arc-minute source grid. Used offline for research;
the original geoid grid is not redistributed.
https://web1.gsi.go.jp/buturisokuchi/grageo_reference.html
https://www.gsi.go.jp/buturisokuchi/grageo_geoidprocedure.html

This is a cross-year geometric reconstruction of a 2018 reference drive and
a 2022 city model, not a reproduction of the exact historical street scene.

Optional live base map: Copyright OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/
OSM Standard raster tiles are requested only for the displayed camera view,
with normal browser caching and visible attribution. They are not bundled
or downloaded for offline use. OSM supplies display context only; the PLATEAU
signal geometry and reference trajectory are not modified using OSM data.
