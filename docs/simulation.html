About the model

Geometry is computed locally in a Web Worker using opaque rectangular buildings. Reflection paths use the image method and are checked for obstruction on both legs. ¹ “No path” means no direct or unobstructed single-bounce path in this model. RF power, diffraction, multiple bounces and position estimation are outside this demo.

Satellite IDs and sky directions are illustrative. Sources are placed 20,200 km from the receiver in local coordinates; rays are shortened for display. The scene uses no real orbit, map, camera or location data. Use the arrow keys to orbit the focused city view, or Space to play and pause the route.

This browser model is separate from the native CUDA simulator and its positioning benchmarks. For captured GPU results on PLATEAU building geometry, open the native simulation viewer ↗.
